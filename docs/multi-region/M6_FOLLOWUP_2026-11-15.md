## Connector transaction deadlock correction

On baseline `71c9779`, a real native missing-row gap-lock barrier across distinct probes reproduced `AcquireRuntime` MariaDB 1213 in 10/10 race-enabled cases. Baseline victim rollback had no partial rows; explicit caller retry recovered, and ownership fences held. This establishes a transient startup retry gap, not data loss. The lifecycle already retried externally after a delay.

The follow-up connector correction retries the complete transaction on typed 1213 only, at most four attempts with cancellable short jitter. Each attempt rereads registration/reset state, database time and ownership. Nondeadlock and ambiguous errors are not retried. Independent review audited all 11 shared-helper callbacks, including prepare/activate; outputs are discarded on terminal errors and there are no external callback effects.

Validation of the corrected code, separate from the earlier offline campaign:
